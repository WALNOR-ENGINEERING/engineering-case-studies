# Engineering problem

Industrial thermal systems may experience:

• uneven temperature distribution  
• excessive thermal accumulation  
• cooling inefficiencies  
• unstable thermal behavior  

These effects influence system performance
and operational reliability.

---

# Analysis overview

The thermal behavior of the engineering system
was analyzed to observe heat transfer
and cooling performance.

---

# Key observations

• thermal accumulation regions  
• uneven heat distribution  
• airflow-cooling interaction  
• thermal gradient behavior  

---

# Proposed improvements

• optimized cooling airflow  
• improved heat dissipation  
• reduced thermal accumulation  
• enhanced thermal stability

Thermal analysis helps improve:
