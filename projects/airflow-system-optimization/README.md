![Cover](cover.png)

# Engineering problem

Industrial airflow systems often contain:

• unstable flow regions  
• excessive turbulence  
• unnecessary pressure losses  
• uneven airflow distribution  

These effects reduce operational efficiency.

---

# Analysis overview

The airflow system was analyzed
to identify problematic regions
and evaluate optimization opportunities.

---

# Key observations

• turbulence accumulation regions  
• unstable airflow transitions  
• localized pressure losses  
• inefficient flow distribution  

---

# Proposed improvements

• smoother airflow transitions  
• geometry optimization  
• turbulence reduction  
• improved flow stability  

---

# Engineering relevance

Optimization analysis helps improve:

• airflow stability  
• energy efficiency

• industrial reliability
